00 · thesis

Messy in. Meaning out.

The interesting problems rarely arrive clean. They arrive as fragmented context, risky transcripts, failing pipelines, noisy markets and ambiguous decisions.

My work is the layer in between: structuring the noise, adding guardrails and shipping a system that holds up in the real world.

Built to survive contact with reality.

Ten systems, ranked for AI-agent work: open-source agent contributions first, then the agent, evaluation and safety systems I build myself. Nine have a live demo you can open in the browser.

  1. 01 open-source agent contributions

    Hermes Agent

    Open-source contributions to the agent from Nous Research.

    problem
    Agents lose context, sessions and routing at the boundaries: a compaction drops history, a session switches mid-turn, a provider alias routes two ways.
    what i built
    Fixes to session recovery, provider routing, scheduler reliability and tool safety, proposed as pull requests and carried into main by the maintainers.
    proof
    27 contributions landed and 34 commits credited on main (21 authored, 13 co-authored), with maintainer credit linked for every one.

  4. 04 voice privacy

    VoiceSafeKit

    A safety gate for voice assistants, before sensitive words reach an LLM.

    problem
    Raw voice transcripts can quietly contain credentials, identity data and private context.
    what i built
    Classifies every transcript as SAFE, REDACT, REVIEW or BLOCK, with browser recording and CLI support.
    proof
    Local-first, open source and built around visible decisions.

    open the demo ↗ source ↗

03 · how i think

Four layers. One useful output.

Great AI is rarely one model. It is a chain of choices about context, structure, constraints and evidence.

  1. 01

    Context

    I begin with the actual inputs, users, risks and constraints, not a model looking for a problem.

    proven in workflowforge · incidentforge
  2. 02

    Structure

    I turn ambiguous material into typed facts, explicit routes and components that can be inspected.

    proven in signalbrief · enterprise ai assistant
  3. 03

    Guardrails

    I design the unsafe, uncertain and human-review paths before the happy path gets impressive.

    proven in voicesafekit · codesentry
  4. 04

    Evidence

    I use evaluation, traces, observability and measurable outcomes to prove the system works outside the demo.

    proven in incidentforge · cognizant delivery
